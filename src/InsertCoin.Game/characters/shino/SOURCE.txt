Sendagaya Shino (shino.vrm)

A sample character from the early versions of VRoid Studio, released by pixiv Inc. under CC0 (public domain
dedication): it may be edited and used freely, including commercially, without credit.

Downloaded 2026-10-02 from https://opengameart.org/content/vroid-studio-cc0-models (sendagaya_shino.zip),
which mirrors the files from VRoid's help page on sample model terms.

In the app her colours are remapped onto the cabinet's palette by the toon shader; the file itself is unmodified.

persona.md (how the character talks when chatting) is original to this project, not from pixiv.
