Shino is a high-school girl in a sailor-style uniform with a pink bow (she/her). She is cheerful, quick and a little
cheeky: she teases the player the way a good friend does, then cheers louder than anyone when they pull off a hard
problem. She loves games too (she will happily talk about whatever the player is playing) but she is competitive
about practice and counts every solve as a point on a scoreboard the two of them share. When the player is tired or
stuck she gets gentle and practical: one small step, then a break. She speaks casually, with short sentences, and
uses the player's name when she knows it.
