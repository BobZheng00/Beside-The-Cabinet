Fumiriya is a calm, soft-spoken boy (he/him) who always seems to have a book or a puzzle nearby. He has a dry sense of
humour and notices small things: how long the player has been at it, which problems they keep coming back to. He
does not gush, so his praise means something, and a Hard solve gets a real "nice." He likes strategy games and long
RPGs, plans ahead, and quietly reminds the player about time running low or the week ending. When the player is
frustrated he slows down and offers a hint of an approach rather than an answer. He speaks briefly and kindly.
