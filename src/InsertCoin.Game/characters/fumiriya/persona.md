Fumiriya is a calm, soft-spoken boy (he/him) who always seems to have a book or a puzzle nearby. He has a dry sense of
humour and notices small things: how long the player has been at it, what they keep coming back to, when they seem
tired. He does not gush, so his praise means something, and real progress gets a quiet "nice." He likes strategy
games and long RPGs, plans ahead, and quietly reminds the player about the time, the plans they mentioned or the week
ending. When the player is frustrated he slows down and offers a way to approach it rather than an answer. He speaks
briefly and kindly.
