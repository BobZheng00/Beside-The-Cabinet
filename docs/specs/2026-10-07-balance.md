# Balance (draft, 2026-10-07)

What the app is for, widened. The user, 2026-10-07: LeetCode and Steam are a small part of it. Any learning or good habit can earn play time, and the aim is a good balance of work, life and games. Talked through with the user the same day. **Draft:** sections marked *proposed* wait for the user's word.

## Where it starts from

- **One way to earn:** accepted LeetCode submissions. Easy pays 1 coin (15 minutes of play), Medium 2, Hard 4, and a star coin on a streak. Focus sessions, standing up and plans she checks in on pay nothing.
- **One way to spend:** Steam games (the registry's `Running` flag).
- **Saving:** coins last until Monday 04:00, when the week's unplayed time expires (`WeeklyReset`, "CASH BOX COLLECTED"). Debt carries over.
- **Setup** asks for a LeetCode username and checks that it exists.
- **Already there for balance:** focus sessions with their breaks, the stand-up nudge, check-ins, quiet hours, the agenda.

## Three ways to earn (agreed)

Sorted by how sure the cabinet can be that it happened. The less it knows, the less it pays.
1. **Checked:** LeetCode, as now. Later, other sites with real APIs, as connectors.
2. **Timed:** focus sessions. They cover any work or study, on the PC or off it (a book, a course, coursework).
3. **Told:** the player's own list of activities, each with its worth, and proof when it asks for some.

The LeetCode username at setup becomes optional.

## Focus pays by the minute (decided; the rate *proposed*)

Focus lengths are the player's choice (5 minutes to 2 hours), so focus pays by the minute, not by the part.
- Minutes count while a focus part runs: not in breaks, not while paused, not while a game is running.
- They add up across parts and sessions, and each time they reach the rate a coin drops. What is left over carries on to the next.
- The FOCUS screen shows how far off the next coin is.
- *Rate, proposed:* a coin (15 minutes of play) for every 60 minutes of focus, 1 to 4. The user asked whether a coin every 25 minutes (3 to 5) was too much; it is. Four hours of real focus, about what a day of deep work holds, then earns an hour of play by itself; LeetCode and activities add to it, up to the daily cap. A setting.

## A daily cap, and unplayed time fades (decided; the numbers *proposed*)

**The cap** is how much play can be earned in a game day (04:00 to 04:00), from everything together.
- *Proposed:* 2 hours; a setting. *Open:* the same every day, or more on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Paying back debt doesn't count towards it: earnings still pay debt first, and the cap limits only new play. Otherwise a deep debt could take days of earning and feel hopeless.
- Past the cap no more coins drop that day. The work still counts in the day's record and the streak, and she says so.

**The fade:** unplayed time decays over days, so it can't be saved up for the weekend.
- *Proposed:* each night at 04:00, half of what wasn't played fades.
- Savings then never pass one day's cap: a day adds at most a cap, and a night halves what there is. So the most play in one day is two days' worth, and only after several days without any. *Stricter, if wanted:* three quarters fade, or all of it.
- It replaces Monday's expiry. Debt never fades. As with the weekly reset, a game running at 04:00 puts the fade off until it ends.
- In the morning the screen shows what faded overnight, and she may mention it.

**The streak** counts the days that earned anything. The week's numbers stay, for her and the attract screen, split by source.

## Activities you tell her about (agreed; details *proposed*)

**The list,** written by the player ahead of time: each activity's name, its worth in coins, and whether it asks for proof (never, sometimes, always). Each counts at most once a day unless set otherwise. For example: the gym 2 coins, a 30-minute walk 1, cooking a proper meal 1.
- A rule of thumb for it: pay for what you want more of but find hard to start, and leave out what you already enjoy, so it stays fun.

**Logging one:**
- on the CRT, in an app with the list as buttons;
- by telling her: she logs it from the list with a tool (`log_activity`), the amount always from the list and never from her, and a tap on her card confirms it, so "I'm going to the gym" isn't taken for "I went";
- at a check-in: something on the agenda can be tied to an activity ("gym at 18:00"), and when she asks how it went and it happened, she logs it.

**Proof** (the user's idea: a photo from outside or a screenshot of the phone's step count, which she judges by how sure she is; fun, not annoying):
- *Sometimes* means she asks now and then, about one time in three; *always*, every time.
- A photo or a screenshot, handed over as any picture is (below). She looks at it as a friend would first: the view, the weather, the steps, the meal. Then she judges:
  - sure: the slip is stamped and the coins drop;
  - unsure: half, said playfully;
  - not convinced: nothing yet, said kindly; a better picture the same day still pays.
- The app tells her what it can check: when a photo was taken, from its metadata when it has some (messaging apps often strip it). A screenshot shows its own date.
- With a model that can't see pictures, proof is skipped and the player's word counts.
- Never a lecture, and never asking twice for the same thing.
- *To check:* reading steps directly. Phones keep them (Apple Health, Android's Health Connect) with no way in from a PC that we know of, so a screenshot is the practical proof.

## Handing her things: pictures and files (agreed; how *proposed*)

Proof needs it, and it is useful on its own.
- **Drop or paste.** A file dropped on the slip, or a picture or file pasted into it (Ctrl+V), shows as a chip in the slip and goes with the next message. Dropped on her, a picture with a character card inside stays a card (as now); any other picture is handed to her.
- **Pictures:** she sees them when the model can; otherwise she says she can't. *To check:* image support through Microsoft.Extensions.AI's `DataContent` in both clients (OpenAI-style and Anthropic).
- **Text and code files** go in as text, up to a size. **PDFs** go as documents to providers that read them, otherwise as their text. *To check.*
- **A screenshot when asked** ("look at my screen"): she asks first with the card, as before acting outside the app, since a screenshot can show anything; then she takes the screen or one window. Never on her own.
- **From the phone** (*proposed*): a QR code on the CRT opens a small page the cabinet serves on the home Wi-Fi; the player picks a photo and it lands in the slip. No account and no cloud; only while the code shows, with a one-time key in it. *To check:* Windows asks once to let the app take connections on the private network.
- **Afterwards** she keeps what she saw in words, not the picture: pictures are large and would cost every later request. The file isn't kept.

## Rest and sleep (agreed; details *proposed*)

**The mechanism is there.** Every 15 seconds the cabinet checks the conditions for check-ins and standing up (`Stool.MaybeCheckIn`, `MaybeStandUp`). A nudge that is due goes on her queue and she says it in her own words once she's free; in a game or full screen it shows as a ticket. Reminders from the agenda are the separate, scheduled path. New nudges join the same loop.

**A record of days,** on this PC only: when the player was first and last at the PC, what they played and when, how long they focused, what they earned and from what. Kept for a few months; theirs to see and clear. She gets a short summary of it, as she gets the cabinet's state. It gives the day's view on the CRT (focused 3h, the gym and a walk, played 1h) and her sense of patterns.

**Bedtime** (the player's own; she asks once if none is set):
- 30 minutes before, a word to start winding down;
- at bedtime, time to sleep;
- once more half an hour later, then quiet. In a game, tickets.
- *Open:* whether play after bedtime costs more, like debt (×1.5).
- Quiet hours still keep check-ins and standing up away; bedtime nudges come regardless, since they are for exactly then.

**Long play:** after two hours in one session she suggests a break: eyes, a stretch, water. In a game, a ticket.

**Patterns she notices,** at most once a day, when the player is back and free, and kindly:
- nights drifting later ("the third night past two");
- long stretches without a break;
- days without play while time is banked: rest counts too.

What the record shows is what is; the bedtime is what the player chose. She never takes a late pattern as the target, though she may suggest getting there in steps.

## Help with learning (agreed; the tools spec's §1, widened)

LeetCode hints become the first case of help with anything being learnt:
- hints in tiers for any subject (a nudge, the approach, an outline; the answer only if asked twice), as a rule in her prompt;
- reading window titles when asked for help, to see what the player is on: a LeetCode tab, a PDF, a course video, their editor;
- LeetCode's problem data as one small built-in source;
- web search and page reading for everything else, built together with it.

## Order (*proposed*, 2026-10-07)

1. This spec.
2. The ledger: focus pays, the daily cap, the nightly fade, the record of days; LeetCode optional at setup.
3. Handing her things: pictures, files, the phone, a screenshot when asked.
4. Activities, with proof.
5. Rest and sleep: bedtime, long play, patterns (by then the record of days holds a few weeks).
6. Help with learning, with web search; opening apps.

The user agreed the first order: this spec, focus pays, activities, then learning with web search. Pictures and files go before activities because proof needs them. Rest and sleep come after, once there is a record to notice patterns in; bedtime alone could come sooner.
